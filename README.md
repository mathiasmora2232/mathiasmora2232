# Hi, I'm Mathias Mora

I build web products, automation tools, and AI-assisted systems from idea to working software.

Right now my work is mostly around full-stack apps, SaaS dashboards, business systems, and practical AI workflows. I like taking messy real-world needs and turning them into something usable: auth, admin panels, booking flows, payments, email, databases, deployment, and the small details that make an app feel complete.

## What I Work On

- Full-stack web apps with TypeScript, JavaScript, Java, PHP, HTML/CSS, and SQL.
- SaaS and business tools for salons, education, marketplaces, and internal operations.
- AI-assisted development workflows with agents, prompts, automation, and repo hygiene.
- Data-backed features: dashboards, user management, reporting, scheduling, and search.
- Product polish: clean UI, usable flows, responsive pages, and practical admin panels.
